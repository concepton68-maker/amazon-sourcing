# Amazon Online/Retail Arbitrage & Sourcing Analysis Tools: Competitive Landscape (as of 2026-11-11)

Method note: about 17 tool calls (vendor pricing pages fetched on 2026-11-11, plus web searches). Chrome Web Store (CWS) and Trustpilot figures come from search-result snapshots, not from listing pages opened today, so treat them as approximate (+/- a few ratings). Reliability codes: **H** = vendor page fetched today (the vendor's own claim, but current); **M** = dated third-party page from 2026, or CWS/Trustpilot via snippet; **L** = affiliate, competitor or advertorial source, or undated. Most comparison sites in this niche (RevenueGeeks, OABeans, AMZMojo, Crowdmob, affninja, thesellingguys) earn affiliate commissions. Vendor-written comparisons (Arbitrage Hero, ProfitPath, Seller Assistant, ASINsider, Plug Seller) are self-serving.

## Q1. Current prices and plan limits per tool (dated, sourced)

### Takeaway
Entry prices for the extension-style "deal analyzers" fall in a narrow band of about $8–55/month: RevSeller $8.33/mo billed annually, AZInsight $7.95–19.95, SellerAmp $19.95–49.95, Seller Assistant $24.99–29.99 at Pro, BuyBotPro $39.95–129.95 USD. Bulk and automated sourcing tools cost much more: Tactical Arbitrage is now sold only inside Threecolts' $69–199 Seller 365 bundle, OAGenius from $79, Zen Arbitrage about $79–97, and ProfitPath automation €159–503. Seller Assistant has moved up-market into a wholesale and workflow suite ($79.99–$999.99+).

## Q3. Feature overlap: table stakes vs differentiators; EU/UK and VAT support; data sources

### Takeaway
Table stakes in 2026 are a fees/ROI/profit calculator, eligibility/restriction checks, IP and hazmat alerts, Keepa-style price/BSR history charts, Buy Box and competition view, Google Sheets export, and a mobile scanner. Differentiators are bulk and automated scanning (Tactical Arbitrage, ProfitPath, Arbitrage Hero, OAGenius), wholesale price-list matching, repricers, supplier discovery with AI, API/MCP/automation integrations (Seller Assistant), and real VAT handling. Only a minority of tools explicitly support EU/UK VAT: BuyBotPro, ProfitPath and ProfitGo, plus SAS and Seller Assistant to some degree. RevSeller is US/CA only.

### Cited Findings
- SAS bundles in every plan: profit calc, eligibility and IP alerts, price and sales charts, Buy Box analysis. — [SellerAmp pricing](https://www.selleramp.com/pricing/) H
- RevSeller has the same core set: FBA/FBM calculator, variations, restriction and eligibility checks, hazmat, IP and meltable alerts, bulk spreadsheet analysis, Sheets export, mobile apps. — [RevSeller](https://revseller.com/) H
- BuyBotPro adds auto-ungating, AI analysis, BuyBotWholesale price-list analysis and Suspension Safeguard. — [BuyBotPro](https://www.buybotpro.com/) H
- Seller Assistant has moved beyond the extension into Bulk Restriction Checker, Price List Analyzer, AI Supplier Finder, Seller Spy, Brand Analyzer, Repricer, Lister, purchase orders and warehouses, prep-center integrations, API, MCP Server, and Zapier/Make/n8n. — [Seller Assistant pricing](https://sellerassistant.app/pricing) H
- Tactical Arbitrage scans retailer sites and wholesale lists. Arbitrage Hero's (competitor) table marks Tactical Arbitrage as not offering a "bulk store scan" in its definition, and marks Seller Assistant as "one product at a time". — [Arbitrage Hero compare](https://arbitragehero.com/compare/) L
- ProfitPath's Wholesale Scanner matches price lists of up to 150,000 rows (CSV, XLSX, PDF) against live Amazon data. Its Supplier Search covers 1,000+ or 2,000+ shops (the vendor is inconsistent). — [ProfitPath](https://profitpath.com/en) H/M via snippet; [ProfitPath blog](https://profitpath.com/en/blog/best-online-arbitrage-tools)

**EU/UK and VAT support:**
- BuyBotPro: UK, US, CA, DE, FR, ES, IT, with AU "coming soon". VAT calculator covers non-registered, standard, flat-rate and US-only sellers. GBP pricing shown ex- and incl.-VAT. — [BuyBotPro](https://www.buybotpro.com/) H

- Seller Assistant: a "VAT Calculator" feature page exists in its navigation, but no marketplace list appears on the pricing page. — [Seller Assistant pricing](https://sellerassistant.app/pricing) H
- RevSeller: .com and .ca only. — [RevSeller](https://revseller.com/) H

**Data sources:**

### Inferences
- Eligibility and restriction checks (which need the seller's SP-API authorisation) and fee calculations (from the SP-API fees estimate) are probably SP-API-based across all tools. Price and BSR history is probably Keepa-licensed or built from each vendor's own collection. This is inference, not verified.
- The EU (non-UK) market is underserved: only BuyBotPro and ProfitPath/ProfitGo explicitly market multi-country EU VAT handling. That is a potential opening.
- Repricers, wholesale list scanning and AI supplier search are where the incumbents are converging. Seller Assistant and Threecolts are bundling toward suites.

### Gaps
- No confirmed per-vendor statement of Keepa vs SP-API vs scraping reliance.
- Amazon Revenue Calculator capabilities were not researched in this pass.

## Q4. Evidence of decline, stagnation, price hikes, acquisitions, churn reasons

### Takeaway
The biggest structural changes since 2025 are these. Tactical Arbitrage was absorbed by Threecolts and is no longer sold standalone; its old URL redirects to a $69–199 ten-app bundle. SourceMogul shut down, changed hands, or was relaunched (sources conflict). Seller Assistant has moved up-market with sharply higher tiers, and its entry plan appears to have risen from about $159.99/yr to $299.99/yr. BuyBotPro and RevSeller show stagnation signals: a broken pricing URL, inconsistent legacy tiers, no copyright year, no UK support for RevSeller, and 0 Trustpilot reviews in 12 months for BuyBotPro. SellerAmp looks stable and actively maintained.

### Cited Findings
- tacticalarbitrage.com/pricing → 301 redirect to threecolts.com/seller-365. A testimonial calls Tactical Arbitrage "now part of the Seller 365 bundle". — [Threecolts](https://www.threecolts.com/seller-365) H
- Seller 365 split into Reseller and Brands bundles in July 2026. — [Threecolts blog](https://www.threecolts.com/blog/seller-365-new-bundles/) M
- SourceMogul: "shut down late 2025" (competitor claim) vs a May 2026 company reply saying it is "still in development" vs a reopening at the end of May 2026. Its pricing page returned 503 on 2026-11-11. — [Arbitrage Hero](https://arbitragehero.com/blog/sourcemogul-alternative/) L; [Trustpilot](https://ca.trustpilot.com/review/sourcemogul.com) M; [SourceMogul pricing](https://www.sourcemogul.com/pricing)
- Seller Assistant's entry price: $159.99/yr cited on its own alternatives page vs Pro at $299.99/yr today. Its Business plan with trial is $79.99/mo. — [alternatives page](https://www.sellerassistant.app/alternatives/buybotpro/) M; [pricing](https://sellerassistant.app/pricing) H
- BuyBotPro: /pricing returns 404, the homepage carries two conflicting tier sets, and Trustpilot shows 0 reviews in the last 12 months. — [BuyBotPro](https://www.buybotpro.com/) H; [Trustpilot IE](https://ie.trustpilot.com/review/buybotpro.com) M
- RevSeller: no dated content, .com/.ca only, "will not yet work for .co.uk". — [RevSeller](https://revseller.com/) H
- SellerAmp: "© 2026 SellerAmp Ltd.", web, mobile and extension apps all current. — [SellerAmp pricing](https://www.selleramp.com/pricing/) H
- Churn and switching drivers mentioned in sources:
  - Bundle cost after Tactical Arbitrage moved to Seller 365 ([Arbitrage Hero](https://arbitragehero.com/blog/tactical-arbitrage-alternatives/) L)
  - Billing, cancellation and refund issues at Seller Assistant ([RevenueGeeks](https://revenuegeeks.com/software/seller-assistant-app) L)
  - Extension load failures at SAS ([CWS reviews](https://chromewebstore.google.com/detail/selleramp-sas-amazon-fba/kidmffepbniamfbibhfgdakkggchipjl/reviews) M)
  - Login issues at BuyBotPro ([Trustpilot AU](https://au.trustpilot.com/review/buybotpro.com) M)
  - SourceMogul unavailability forcing migration ([Arbitrage Hero](https://arbitragehero.com/blog/sourcemogul-alternative/) L)
- A 2026 comparison notes that most solo sellers pick one analyzer rather than running several. — [ASINsider](https://asinsider.com/blog/selleramp-vs-seller-assistant) L

### Inferences
- The market is consolidating (Threecolts roll-up) and the remaining independents are splitting into suites (Seller Assistant), which leaves room for focused, cheaper, EU-aware tools. Arbitrage Hero and ProfitPath are positioning exactly there.
- Single-extension analyzers (SAS, RevSeller, AZInsight, BuyBotPro) look commoditised: identical feature sets and $8–55 price points.

### Gaps
- No Reddit threads were retrieved, so first-hand churn sentiment remains unsampled. A targeted Reddit search would strengthen this section.
- No independent confirmation of SourceMogul's ownership change.
- No historical SAS or BuyBotPro price series to confirm or rule out price hikes.
- Amazon's free Revenue Calculator as a substitute threat was not assessed.

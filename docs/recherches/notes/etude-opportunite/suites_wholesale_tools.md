## 1. Helium 10: pricing, 2024–2026 changes, ownership, ratings gap

### Takeaway
List prices on helium10.com, fetched 2026-11-11, match the brief: Platinum $129/mo monthly or $99/mo annual, Diamond $359/mo or $279/mo, Enterprise from $1,499/mo billed annually. In 2026 Helium 10 removed the Starter plan, cut Platinum entitlements, moved Helium 10 Ads (PPC) to Diamond only, added a 2% fee on managed ad spend, and launched an AI agent and an MCP connector (Diamond-gated or paid add-on). Those changes line up with the 2026 Trustpilot complaints. This most likely explains much of the gap between Trustpilot 2.0 (717 reviews) and G2 ~4.2 (~184 reviews).

### Cited Findings
**Pricing and limits (official page, fetched 2026-11-11) [H]** — [Helium 10 pricing](https://www.helium10.com/pricing/)
- Platinum: $129/mo monthly, $99/mo annual ("saves $360/yr"). Positioned for $0–100K in sales.
- Diamond: $359/mo monthly, $279/mo annual ("saves $960/yr"). Positioned for $100K–10M.
- Enterprise: "$1,499 starting price", billed annually, for sales beyond $10M. Includes a dedicated CSM, ad budgets of $25K–1M/mo, weekly sessions and 1:1 coaching.
- Limits, Platinum vs Diamond:
  - Market Tracker: 3 vs 5 markets.
  - ASINs tracked (alerts): 20 vs 1,000 lifetime (Enterprise up to 5,000).
  - Seller Central connections: 2 vs 10 (Enterprise 15+).
  - Listing Analyzer: 50 vs 150 per month.
  - Fraud detector: 5 vs 200 products.
  - Follow-up emails: 5,000 vs 15,000.
  - Inventory: 40 vs 500 SKUs.
  - Influencer finder: 10 vs 250 per day.
  - Managed refund fee: 15% vs 10%.
  - Users: 1 vs 5 additional users (Enterprise 10+).
- Keyword search limit: "100 searches monthly" for competitive keyword discovery; the page does not say which plan. Keyword Tracker is an add-on from $19/mo.
- Diamond only: Helium Agent ("Keyword, Listing, Ads, and Operations intelligence in one conversation"), Helium 10 Ads, AI Listing Builder integration, rules-based ad automation and dayparting, inventory management, P&L reporting, long-term keyword trends.
- MCP: "Helium MCP" is included free on Diamond up to 1,000 calls. Other plans pay for an MCP add-on from $50/mo.
- Ad fee: "Diamond plan customers incur a 2% management fee on PPC spend managed through Helium 10 Ads."
- Trial: none on the pricing page. Refunds: no policy stated. Monthly plans carry "no long-term commitments" and auto-renew.

**Timeline of changes**
- Platinum was $97–99/mo monthly before 2026. Monthly Platinum rose to $129 and monthly Diamond rose from $279 to $359 [M] — [BuyBoxStack](https://buyboxstack.com/blog/helium-10-pricing), [SellerForge](https://www.sellerforge.ai/blog/is-helium-10-worth-it), [BagEngine](https://bagengine.com/articles/helium-10-review) (affiliate or competitor review sites).
- Conflict on annual pricing: [RevenueGeeks](https://revenuegeeks.com/helium-10-pricing/) says annual Diamond rose from $229 to $279; SellerForge says annual prices held [L].
- The $39/mo Starter plan was discontinued for new subscribers. One source dates this to April 2026, others to January 2026 [M on the fact, L on the date] — [SentryKit, 2026-06-08](https://sentrykit.com/blog/helium-10-price-increase-2026-starter-plan-removed/). SentryKit sells a competing Buy Box monitor, so it is biased.
- From 2026-04-01, per EcomCrew as relayed by SentryKit, Platinum lost entitlements [L, second-hand]:
  - Cerebro and Magnet capped at 100 searches/month.
  - Listing Builder AI capped at 5 lifetime uses.
  - Helium 10 Ads removed.
  - Alerts capped at 5 ASINs lifetime. Today's official page shows 20 ASINs for Platinum, so this cap appears to have changed since April or the report was imprecise.
  - Source: [SentryKit](https://sentrykit.com/blog/helium-10-price-increase-2026-starter-plan-removed/)
- "Helium 10 Ads moved to Diamond only; Platinum no longer includes the PPC tool" [H, confirmed by the official FAQ] — [Helium 10 pricing](https://www.helium10.com/pricing/)
- Free tier status is unclear: some sources say a limited free plan still exists, others say it is gone [L] — [enjoy-aiia](https://enjoy-aiia.com/helium-10-pricing/). It was not visible on the official grid.

**Ownership [M]**
- Assembly acquired Helium 10 in 2020. Assembly, which is backed by private equity, rebranded as Pacvue in 2023 (reportedly 2023-03-27), and Helium 10 continued under Pacvue — [RevenueGeeks owner page](https://revenuegeeks.com/software/helium-10/owner), [eMarketer (~2023)](https://www.emarketer.com/content/pacvue-wants-solve-retail-media-s-fragmentation-problem).
- Answer to the brief: Helium 10 is owned by Assembly, which now operates as Pacvue (so both names in the brief are correct).
- No 2026 ownership change was found. No Carbon6 link was found; Carbon6 was acquired by SPS Commerce, a deal that closed in Feb 2025 for about $144.9M — [SPS Commerce 10-Q FY2026](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001092699/000109269926000043/spsc-20260331.htm) [H].
- Deal values and Pacvue financials: see Gaps.

**Adoption [L]**
- The Chrome extension is marketed as "trusted by over 4,000,000 sellers". This is a self-declared marketing figure, and Helium 10 publishes no audited seller count — [Spocket review](https://www.spocket.co/blogs/helium-10-review).

**Ratings**
- Trustpilot, fetched 2026-11-11 [H] — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/helium10.com):
  - TrustScore 2.0/5 ("Poor"), 717 reviews, of which 48 came in the last 12 months.
  - Star split: 5★ 54%, 4★ 3%, 3★ 2%, 2★ 3%, 1★ 38% (strongly polarized).
  - Trustpilot flags that the company does not reply to negative reviews and has not recently invited reviews.
- G2: 4.2/5 from 184 reviews per the G2 page snippet. Other sites cite 4.1 from 170–180 reviews [M] — [G2](https://www.g2.com/products/helium-10/reviews) (snippet only; direct fetch returned 403), [rfp.wiki](https://www.rfp.wiki/retail-ecommerce/online-marketplace-optimization-tools/helium-10/sellerapp), [ecom-tools.de](https://ecom-tools.de/en/helium-10-review/).
- Capterra: about 4.2–4.3 from roughly 100–110 reviews, per secondary sites only [L] — [Capterra reviews](https://www.capterra.com/p/193503/Helium10/reviews/), [DemandSage](https://www.demandsage.com/helium-10-review/).

**Documented 2026 Trustpilot complaints (likely churn reasons) [H for the review content, L for representativeness]** — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/helium10.com)
- Price hike with no new features (2026-05-05: Platinum went from $99 to $129).
- Platinum "stripped" to push users toward Diamond (2026-10-14).
- A 2% ad-spend fee added without clear notice (2026-07-27).
- Refunds refused after renewal: credits offered instead (2026-10-28); refund denied after re-billing (2026-10-20); annual renewal charged with no reminder (2026-07-20).
- Data missing after reactivation (2026-07-08).
- Account data deleted and support replying only with AI-generated answers (2026-07-13).
- Praise: fast support (2026-09-01); "tools are fine" (2026-04-04).

### Inferences
- **Why Trustpilot and G2 disagree:**
  - (a) The two platforms collect differently. G2 relies on solicited, verified B2B reviews, often gathered through incentive campaigns. Trustpilot is open to anyone, and Helium 10 has stopped inviting reviews there, so new Trustpilot reviews come mostly from angry users.
  - (b) The billing and cancellation pain (annual auto-renewals, no refunds) and the 2026 de-bundling of Platinum hit exactly the users who write unsolicited reviews.
  - (c) The 54% five-star share likely comes from older invitation campaigns. Only 48 of the 717 reviews are from the last 12 months.
  - (d) Not replying to negative reviews lowers the TrustScore further.
  - Overall: a pricing and billing problem, not a data-quality problem.
- Strategy: Helium 10 is moving upmarket. The entry price rose to $99–129/mo, high-value features (Ads, AI agent, MCP quota) sit in Diamond, and Pacvue covers enterprise retail media. This leaves price-sensitive beginners and small sellers underserved.

### Gaps
- Exact effective dates of each 2026 Helium 10 change: no official changelog found, and the Wayback Machine was not checked.
- Pacvue/Assembly funding, valuation and revenue: Crunchbase and PitchBook pages were not readable.
- Similarweb traffic for helium10.com was not retrieved (the Similarweb connector is not authorized).
- Whether a free plan still exists.

## 2. Jungle Scout: pricing, ownership, layoffs, Cobalt, AI/MCP/API

### Takeaway
Jungle Scout now sells two lines:
- **Catalyst:** a self-serve tool for sellers with under $1M in Amazon revenue. Its tiers are Starter, Growth Accelerator and Brand Owner, and it has a 7-day money-back guarantee and no free trial.
- **Cobalt:** enterprise market intelligence with custom per-user pricing.

In September 2026 it launched "Ask Cobalt" and a Jungle Scout MCP, sold through demos. It remains independent; no acquisition was found. CEO is Jay Lovelace since 2024. Layoffs are confirmed only for late 2023.

### Cited Findings
**Pricing**
- From the pricing page, fetched 2026-11-11 [H] — [Jungle Scout pricing](https://www.junglescout.com/pricing/):
  - Catalyst is for "companies selling less than $1M" and has 3 plans.
  - Starter is "limited to 1 user". Growth Accelerator includes 1 user, Brand Owner 3 users. Extra seats cost $49/mo or $459/yr.
  - Catalyst tracks up to 200 ASINs, offers "up to 140" metrics and covers 8 marketplaces.
  - Cobalt tracks up to 20,000 ASINs, offers "over 800" metrics and covers 19 marketplaces. It is priced per user with a dedicated CSM.
  - "We do not offer a free trial"; there is a "7-day money back guarantee on our standard plans".
  - The fetched page did not show base prices for the Catalyst tiers.
- Starter pricing reported by third parties: $49/mo monthly or $29/mo annual ($348/yr). No reliable Growth Accelerator or Brand Owner price was found [L] — [bloggersideas](https://www.bloggersideas.com/?p=2163319), [PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/junglescout).
- Plan history: Basic, Suite and Professional were renamed to Starter, Growth Accelerator and Brand Owner around Feb 2026, and Catalyst's target segment was narrowed from $0–5M to $0–1M in revenue around June 2026 [L] — [Costbench changelog](https://costbench.com/changelog/catalyst-plans-renamed-2026-02/), [PricingSaaS 2026W26 diff](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/junglescout/diffs/2026W26).

**AI, MCP and API**
- Press release dated 2026-09-10 [H] — [EIN Presswire](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/941275851/jungle-scout-launches-ai-solutions-putting-amazon-insights-just-a-question-away):
  - Launched "Jungle Scout AI Solutions": Ask Cobalt (natural-language Q&A inside Cobalt) and Jungle Scout MCP (works in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini Enterprise and Microsoft Copilot).
  - Pricing is not disclosed and access is via demo. CEO Jay Lovelace is quoted.
- The MCP product page also routes access through a demo or a customer-success contact [H] — [Jungle Scout MCP](https://www.junglescout.com/products/jungle-scout-mcp/).
- Jungle Scout offers an API "to build self-serve tools"; plan inclusion is not stated [H] — [pricing FAQ](https://www.junglescout.com/pricing/).

**Adoption claims (self-declared) [H, as claims]** — [press release, 2026-09-10](https://www.einpresswire.com/article/941275851/jungle-scout-launches-ai-solutions-putting-amazon-insights-just-a-question-away)

Lovelace is still CEO per the Sep 2026 release.
- Layoffs: Sourcing Journal/WWD reported that Jungle Scout laid off an unconfirmed number of employees, apparently in late 2023 [M] — [WWD/Sourcing Journal](https://wwd.com/sourcing-journal/layoffs-fourkites-etsy-zulily-job-cuts-gm-cruise-sps-ventures-jungle-scout-1238826539/). Glassdoor reviews describe repeated restructuring and layoff rounds; this is anecdotal [L] — [Glassdoor](https://www.glassdoor.com/Reviews/Employee-Review-Jungle-Scout-E1870851-RVW88656659.htm).

**Ratings**
- Trustpilot, fetched 2026-11-11 [H] — [Trustpilot](https://www.trustpilot.com/review/junglescout.com):
  - TrustScore 4.0/5 from 3,940 reviews, but only 9 in the last 12 months.

### Inferences
- The 4.0 TrustScore is "stale goodwill": almost all reviews predate 2025, and recent ones are mostly negative. Low recent review volume also suggests the self-serve user base has shrunk relative to its peak. That is plausible, but not verified.
- Strategic shift: Jungle Scout now focuses on Cobalt (enterprise, sales-led, demo-only AI/MCP) and narrowed Catalyst to under $1M sellers. This mirrors Helium 10's move upmarket and leaves the self-serve mid-market less contested on AI and MCP access.

## 3. SmartScout: pricing, brand/seller database, data source

### Takeaway
SmartScout raised prices around September 2026:
- Basic: $49/mo (from $29); about $29/mo on annual.
- Essentials: $119/mo; $99 annual.
- Business: $299/mo; $239 annual.
- Enterprise: custom.

All self-serve plans carry a 7-day money-back guarantee. Its differentiator is brand-, seller- and subcategory-level data (30M+ products, 500K+ brands, 2M+ sellers with buy box history). Sales estimates are rank-based and partly cross-checked against the Keepa API.

## 4. Keepa: end-user subscription and role as the foundational data layer

### Takeaway
Keepa's paid "data access" subscription rose from €19 to €29/mo (€290/yr) on about 2026-02-03, without grandfathering. The API is a separate plan from €49/mo (20 tokens/min) up to several thousand euros per month. Keepa is the de facto shared data layer: price, sales-rank and buy-box history used by wholesale scanners (Scan Unlimited shows Keepa charts) and by intelligence tools (SmartScout cross-checks against the Keepa API).

### Cited Findings
- Price change: from €19 to €29 (about $32), "52.6% increase, no grandfathering", Keepa update of "February 3rd". The article is dated 2026-02-18. It also says the free tier was "gutted" (details paywalled) [M] — [officialolsons Substack, 2026-02-18](https://officialolsons.substack.com/p/keepa-just-changed-the-rules-heres). €19/mo was the price as of Jan 2023 [M] — [AMZFinder](https://www.amzfinder.com/blog/keepa-pricing/).
- Current plan: €29/mo or €290/yr. Billed in euros. No free trial for the paid plan [M] — [RevenueGeeks](https://revenuegeeks.com/keepa-pricing/), [SellerTrove](https://sellertrove.com/keepa-pricing), [AMZFinder](https://www.amzfinder.com/blog/keepa-pricing/).
- Free vs paid: the free account has price-history graphs, tracking and price alerts. Sales rank, Buy Box and offer-count history sit behind the paywall [M] — [AMZFinder](https://www.amzfinder.com/blog/keepa-pricing/).
- API [M/L] — [RevenueGeeks Keepa API](https://revenuegeeks.com/software/keepa/api), [Nexscope](https://www.nexscope.ai/blog/keepa-api-guide):
  - Priced by tokens per minute: €49 for 20 tokens/min, €129 for 60, €459 for 250, €879 for 500, up to about €4,499 for 4,000. One source cites a top tier of €11,099.
  - No free API tier.
  - The €29 data-access plan includes 1 token/min.
- Keepa.com blocks automated fetching, so the official price could not be verified directly (fetch attempt returned an empty page) [H on the failure].
- Downstream dependence:
  - Scan Unlimited's plans list "Keepa Charts with Sales Rank History" [H] — [Scan Unlimited pricing](https://www.scanunlimited.com/pricing).
  - SmartScout cross-references the Keepa API [M] — see §3.

### Inferences
- Keepa raised end-user prices by about 50% and tightened its free tier in 2026. Every tool that resells or depends on Keepa data faces rising input costs: a large scanner needs the €459–€879+/mo API tiers. Combined with the parallel price increases at Helium 10 and SmartScout, this points to tightening margins in the ecosystem.
- No official Keepa ToS or API-terms change was verified.

### Gaps
- Official Keepa pricing page content.
- Details of the free-tier reduction and any API ToS changes from the 2026-02 update (paywalled).
- Keepa user counts. Chrome Web Store figures were not retrieved.
- Keepa ownership (a private company; nothing checked).

## 5. Wholesale analyzers: pricing, scan volumes, limits, speed

### Takeaway
Bulk wholesale-file analyzers are cheap, commoditized tools ($39–$190/mo). They differ mainly in row/scan caps, restriction (ungating) checks and day passes:
- **Scan Unlimited:** a free plan plus $70/mo for 100K rows per file.
- **Analyzer.Tools:** $49–69/mo plus day passes; its Windows app is being sunset.
- **Seller Assistant:** $29.99–$189.99/mo with metered scans; no refunds.
- **Tactical Arbitrage:** about $59–159/mo, now packaged inside Threecolts' Seller 365.

### Cited Findings
**Scan Unlimited (official, fetched 2026-11-11) [H]** — [Scan Unlimited pricing](https://www.scanunlimited.com/pricing)
- Limited (free): 1 upload/month, 10,000 products per file.
- Unlimited: $70/mo, or $60/mo on the annual plan ("2 months free"). Unlimited uploads, 100,000 products per file.
- Claimed speed on both plans: "300,000+ products/hour".
- "The free plan is essentially a perpetual free trial." No refund policy stated.
- Requires an Amazon Professional seller account. The pricing page still says it uses "MWS credentials", but MWS has been deprecated in favor of SP-API, which suggests the page is stale.
- A third party lists "Unlimited Plus" at $1,080/yr. Not on the official page today [L] — [RevenueGeeks](https://revenuegeeks.com/software/scanunlimited).

**Analyzer.Tools (official, fetched 2026-11-11) [H]** — [Analyzer.Tools pricing](https://www.analyzer.tools/pricing)
- Windows App: $39/mo, labeled "Sunset". Web app: $49/mo. Web app Pro: $69/mo.
- Day passes: $15 for 2 days, $25 for 5 days. Pay-per-burst pricing like this suits occasional supplier-list checks.
- No annual price, row limits, trial or refund terms are shown.
- Published bulk ungating check [L] — [Seller Assistant blog](https://www.sellerassistant.app/blog/best-amazon-bulk-analysis-scanning-tools/) (competitor).

**Seller Assistant (official, fetched 2026-11-11) [H]** — [Seller Assistant pricing](https://sellerassistant.app/pricing)
- Pro: $29.99/mo ($24.99 on annual). Business: $79.99 ($69.99). Business Plus: $189.99 ($159.99). Agency: from $999.99, annual contract only.
- Price List Analyzer scans per month: Pro 3,000; Business 150K (300K on annual); Business Plus and Agency unlimited. Add-on: +50K UPC/EAN scans.
- Bulk Restriction Checker: Pro 4K (6K on annual); Business 20K (50K on annual); unlimited at the top tiers.
- 14-day trial on Business only; trial exports are capped at 25 rows.
- "We do not offer refunds as our service is subscription-based."
- Covers wholesale, online arbitrage and dropshipping. The broadest workflow of the group (sourcing, eligibility checks, purchase orders) [L] — [Seller Assistant blog](https://www.sellerassistant.app/blog/best-amazon-price-list-analyzers/) (vendor, biased).

**Tactical Arbitrage (Threecolts) [L]**
- Six monthly plans from $59 to $159; the Wholesale plan is about $69/mo — [RevenueGeeks](https://revenuegeeks.com/software/tactical-arbitrage/pricing) (affiliate, updated Jul–Aug 2026).

### Inferences

  - (3) Entry tiers removed or narrowed: Helium 10 Starter gone; Jungle Scout Catalyst narrowed to under $1M sellers.
  - (4) Every player is now shipping MCP/AI, usually gated to high tiers (Helium 10 Diamond, SmartScout Business/Enterprise, Jungle Scout Cobalt demo-only) or sold as a paid add-on (Helium 10 $50+/mo, AMZScout $39+/mo).

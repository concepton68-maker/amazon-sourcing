Conventions: "monthly" means month-to-month billing, and "annual" means the per-month equivalent with annual billing. ## Q1. Which products already combine sourcing (product research) AND account management (profit, inventory, accounting, reimbursements, PPC)? What does each plan cost, including add-ons?

### Takeaway
Four vendors verifiably sell **both halves in a single subscription**:
- **Threecolts Seller 365 Reseller**, from $69/mo or $65/mo annual. It covers sourcing (Tactical Arbitrage, ScoutIQ, Scoutify) plus InventoryLab, accounting, profits, a repricer and reimbursements at a 15% commission.
- **Jungle Scout Catalyst Growth Accelerator**, $79/mo or $49/mo annual. It includes research plus Sales Analytics, Inventory Manager and advertising analytics.
- **Seller Assistant (sellerassistant.app) Business**, $79.99/mo or $69.99/mo annual. It covers wholesale/OA sourcing plus purchase orders, landed cost, a repricer and Profitability (beta).
- **Helium 10 Platinum**, $129/mo or $99/mo annual. It includes research plus "limited" profits, 40-SKU inventory, Follow-Up and a managed refund service at 15%. PPC (Helium 10 Ads) is only in Diamond at $359/mo or $279/mo annual, plus a 2% fee on ad spend.

Pure research tools sell no account management: SellerAmp, SmartScout, AMZScout, and ZonGuru (now "Helix", focused on listings). Pure management tools sell no sourcing: Sellerboard, Sellerise, SoStocked, GoAura, BQool and Forecastly.

## Q3. Does any tool provide a closed loop: sourcing decision (planned ROI at purchase) → inventory → realized profit per purchase batch, with planned vs actual comparison?

### Takeaway
**No tool, on the evidence gathered, explicitly documents a "planned ROI vs realized ROI per purchase batch" comparison.** Three tools come close, and each covers part of the chain:
- **Seller Assistant** carries analysis data into the purchase order and reports realized profit. Its P&L is grouped by product, brand and supplier, not by PO.
- **InventoryLab** records buy cost, purchase date and supplier per line. Its P&L uses the transaction posting date.
- **Sellerboard** applies COGS by FIFO batch. It has no sourcing and no "planned" ROI.

### Cited Findings
- **Seller Assistant (app):**
  - "Data from your analysis carries into the PO, so nothing is re-entered" and "See fees, profit, and ROI per SKU before the order ships" (at the PO stage).
  - Landed cost is "Weighted across every PO the SKU appeared in" and "Your actual landed cost, not an estimate".
  - Profitability: "**Know the profit you actually made, not the profit you planned**". It covers "Sales, Amazon fees, refunds, ads, and your purchase order costs in one P&L, by product, brand, and supplier".
  - **There is no explicit planned-vs-actual view per PO.** Landed cost is a weighted average across POs, not a batch-level figure.
  — [sellerassistant.app](https://sellerassistant.app/)
- **InventoryLab (Stratify, now "InventoryLab"):**
  - While listing, you enter "Avg Cost/Unit", "Purchased" (date) and "Supplier". You can enter an "ideal ROI" so that the list price is adjusted.
  - **No report comparing expected ROI with realized profit is documented.**
  — [InventoryLab KB – Accounting While Listing](https://thjrecvolts.useresponse.com/knowledge-base/article/accounting-while-listing) (UseResponse KB that references InventoryLab/Stratify; the publisher is not stated on the page)
  - Multiple buy costs per MSKU are supported (separate lines by supplier, cost and date). — [KB – Multiple Buy Costs](https://thjrecvolts.useresponse.com/knowledge-base/article/enter-multiple-buy-costs-during-list)
  - The Supplier Profitability report shows ROI by supplier for the orders in the period. — [KB – Supplier Profitability](https://thjrecvolts.useresponse.com/knowledge-base/article/supplier-profitability)
  - The P&L counts transactions by posting date. — [KB – Profit & Loss](https://thjrecvolts.useresponse.com/knowledge-base/article/profit-loss)
  - Threecolts marketing line: "Know exactly what you're making – before you buy and after you sell". It does not describe a comparison view. — [Threecolts – InventoryLab](https://www.threecolts.com/seller-365/inventorylab)
- **Seller 365:** Tactical Arbitrage, ScoutIQ and Scoutify (sourcing with ROI) are bundled with InventoryLab and Accounting in the same subscription. However, the official page does not describe any automatic transfer of a "planned ROI" to an actual purchase lot. — [Seller 365](https://www.threecolts.com/seller-365)
- **Sellerboard:**
  - COGS "By period/batch/marketplace", with an "Accounting method" of FIFO, weighted average or by period.
  - Each batch has a receipt date, quantity and cost per unit.
  - "sellerboard applies the cost of the oldest stock to your earliest sales". Historical orders are recalculated.
  - Multi-product FIFO batches can be created from a shipment or "Send to Amazon".
  - **No sourcing and no planned ROI.**
  — [Sellerboard blog – FIFO](https://blog.sellerboard.com/2025/09/21/🎯-smarter-profit-tracking-with-fifo-new-cogs-method-now-in-sellerboard/); [Sellerboard blog – Nov 2025 updates](https://blog.sellerboard.com/2025/11/11/smarter-workflows-clearer-stock-faster-insights-four-new-updates-in-sellerboard/)
- **Helium 10 and Jungle Scout:** their pricing pages show no link between the research tools (Black Box, Product Database) and a "planned ROI" stored per purchase. Profits and Sales Analytics are P&L tools based on COGS. — [Helium 10 Pricing](https://www.helium10.com/pricing/); [JS Catalyst](https://www.junglescout.com/pricing/catalyst-plans)
- **SellerAmp:** History logs analyses only, not purchases, and there is no profit tracking. — [SellerAmp features](https://selleramp.com/features/)

### Inferences
- The closest to a closed loop is **Seller Assistant Business ($69.99–79.99/mo)**: sourcing analysis → PO with ROI → landed cost → realized P&L. The missing piece is an explicit **planned (PO) vs realized comparison per PO or batch**.
- In the Threecolts suite, a closed loop is technically possible in a single subscription (TA/Scoutify → InventoryLab buy cost by date and supplier). The comparison is still not automated: the user would have to compare by hand.
- This "planned vs actual per batch" gap is the most clearly unfilled differentiator in the market, based on the evidence collected. Its absence is not proven: there are no help-center articles saying "not supported".

### Gaps
